Executive Branch

The Executive Board

Five elected officers lead SGA.

President

Chief representative of the student body. Sets SGA’s priorities for the year and serves as the primary liaison to WPI administration.

sgapresident@wpi.edu

Vice President

Manages Senate operations and committee assignments, keeping the legislative side of SGA running.

sgavp@wpi.edu

Secretary

Keeps SGA’s records, publishes meeting minutes, and handles official communications.

sgasecretary@wpi.edu

Treasurer

Oversees SGA finances and chairs the Financial Board, which decides how student activity funds are allocated.

sgatreasurer@wpi.edu

Chief Justice

Leads the Judicial Branch and interprets SGA’s governing documents when their meaning or application is in question.

Legislative Branch

Committees

Committees are grouped into three clusters. Every chair sits on the Cabinet.

Committee on Student Affairs — researches student life and academic issues, and brings recommendations to Senate. If something about living or studying at WPI is broken, this is the committee that investigates it.

sgastaffairschair@wpi.edu

  • Community Outreach — runs events and builds collaborations across campus and with the surrounding Worcester community. sgacochair@wpi.edu
  • Marketing — owns SGA’s social media and publicity, including @wpi.sga. sgamarketchair@wpi.edu
  • Tech Coordinator — an appointed position supporting SGA’s digital tools and this website.

  • Appropriations — reviews and recommends funding allocations. sgaappropchair@wpi.edu
  • Fiscal Responsibility and Assistance — helps organizations manage their budgets responsibly. sgafrachair@wpi.edu
  • Fiscal Education — trains club treasurers and leaders on how SGA funding actually works. sgafedchair@wpi.edu
  • Accountant and Financial Board Secretary — appointed positions supporting the Financial Board.
The Senate

Who sits in Senate

The Senate is made up of:

  • Senators
  • Honorary Senators non-voting
  • Committee Chairs
  • Organizational Representatives
  • Executive Branch members non-voting

How to get a seat

Key bodies

Cabinet & Financial Board

The Cabinet brings together every committee chair, coordinating work across the three clusters.

The Financial Board makes funding decisions for recognized student organizations, chaired by the Treasurer.

Quorum: a simple majority for most bodies, but two-thirds for financial matters.

SGA

The official voice of the WPI undergraduate student body. Senate meets Tuesdays at 6:00 PM in Innovation Studio 203 — open to everyone.
