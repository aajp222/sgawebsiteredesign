Get set up

Workday and treasurer basics

  • Every club needs one treasurer who is on campus during their term of service. If your treasurer will be away (for example during an IQP), an interim treasurer needs to step in.
  • The treasurer oversees the club's funding, so they should be comfortable with everything on this page.
  • All club funds need to be in your club's Workday account. SAO's financial policies (linked under Forms and links below) have the details.

Questions about any of this can go to the Fiscal Education Chair at sgafedchair@wpi.edu.

Treasurers need Workday access. Update your club's Treasurer on myWPI by the first week of the term, and Workday updates automatically. If something looks off, email the Student Activities Office (SAO) at gr-sao@wpi.edu.

  • Each club has one Treasurer at a time.
  • Treasurers have to be on campus during the terms they serve. If yours will be away for a term or longer, name an Interim Treasurer in myWPI and Workday.

Search for your student organization in Workday's main search bar. It shows up with a code like XXX-AG, and that is your tag. This works for any student organization, not just yours.

Your tag connects to your club's WPI Workday account, and you will use it on forms like the 25Live event form and when sending or receiving IDTs.

  • SAO policy is that all club money lives in your WPI Workday account. Clubs cannot have an off-campus bank account unless they are affiliated with a national organization that provides a fidelity bond.
  • Clubs can never hold more than $100 in cash at a time.
  • You use the account to pay internal charges (Facilities, Chartwells, LNL, and so on) and bills from outside vendors.
Spending

Spending your club's money

Submit expense reports, supplier invoices, or other payments as soon as you can. It is fine for your balance to go negative if a Funding Request was approved and you have not submitted the report form yet. If you are spending funding request money, you still have to submit the FR Report Form with receipts in addition to any Workday paperwork.

Which payment method to use
If...Use...
You paid with your own moneyAn Expense Report in Workday (or the paper form if you do not have Workday access)
A supplier sent you an invoiceA Supplier Invoice Request in Workday, if the supplier is an approved WPI supplier
You are ordering suppliesA Requisition in Workday, or Amazon Business through Workday for Amazon orders
You need to pay another club, or get paid by oneAn IDT (interdepartmental transfer) form
Something elseContact SAO, or use your advisor's procurement card (P-Card)

Expense reports reimburse someone who spent personal money for the club. Treasurers and student workers usually have Workday access and can use the online version.

With Workday access

  1. The person being reimbursed searches Workday for "Expense Report".
  2. They fill in the required information with a descriptive name in "Memo", and put your club's Workday tag in "Student Organization".
  3. On the next page they add their receipts and submit.
  4. SAO approves it, then it goes to the club Treasurer to approve. Finance then processes it.

Without Workday access

  • Have them fill out the Student Activities Expense Report from the Controller's Office forms page and send it to the Treasurer. They must sign it, not just type their name.
  • The Treasurer checks it, signs and prints their name, and sends it to SAO at gr-sao@wpi.edu with the receipts.

Use these to pay a supplier directly from your club's Workday account once they send you an invoice. The supplier has to be an Approved WPI Supplier. If yours is not, there is a form to get them added. There is a more detailed tutorial in the WPI Hub.

  1. In Workday, search for "supplier invoice request" and create one.
  2. Fill in the Supplier and Currency. Common vendors are already in the system.
  3. Add the invoice's line items. You will usually pick "Service Line" unless you are buying goods, and then choose a Spend Category. Make the Extended Amount match the Control Total Amount.
  4. Put your club's Workday tag in "Student Organization". Cost Center and Fund fill in on their own.
  5. Attach a PDF of the invoice.

WPI Procurement has an agreement that lets clubs buy from Amazon straight through Workday, with free shipping and tax exemption built in. The invoice goes to your club's Workday account, so there is no expense report to file. Your Treasurer needs Workday access.

  1. In Workday, go to the Purchases tab and click "Connect to Supplier Website".
  2. Set Requisition Type to "General" and type your club's name in "Student Organization", then press Enter.
  3. Click OK. On the next page, click "Connect" next to Amazon Business.
  4. You land in Amazon Business and check out like normal.

Requisitions: see the PO/Requisition tutorial.

IDTs: to send money to another club or ask for money from one, fill out the Interdepartmental Transfer/Journal Entry Form on the Controller's Office forms page. You need the other club's Workday tag, and you have to fill out the credit and debit amounts correctly.

Tax exemption: WPI is tax exempt, so many club purchases (including anything through Amazon Business) can be made without sales tax. Contact SAO or SGA about any purchase that is not going through Workday Punchout or a P-Card.

Income

Bringing money in

SGA does not guarantee funding from any of these, and each fund goes to organizations whose mission fits its purpose.

  • SOAP Fund: a mini-grant from the Campus Center and SAO for campus-wide social and educational programming, usually up to $250 per program. The event has to be on a Tuesday, Friday, or Saturday night, be open to all of campus, and be applied for at least a month ahead. Apply with the SOAP Substance Free Programming Fund Mini-Grant Application on the SAO myWPI page.
  • Graduate Student Government (GSG): for events that serve graduate students. Apply on Techsync at least two weeks before the event. GSG's executive board decides the amount.
  • Department funding: decided case by case. Contact the head of the relevant academic department.
  • Office of Diversity, Inclusion, and Multicultural Education (ODIME): typically funds part of the cost for events that fit. Submit the request form on their website at least 4 weeks ahead.
  • Sponsorships: reach out to local businesses to see if they would sponsor your event.

All SAO-registered clubs in Classes I, II, III, IV, and VI can fundraise. Fundraisers help cover costs that SGA cannot, such as raffles, bingo, casino nights, and table-sitting in the Campus Center.

  • Register every fundraiser with SAO using the SAO Fundraiser Request Form.
  • Many groups have people pay through the myWPI store.
  • WPI's annual giving day, Love Your Club Day, is in September and lets clubs ask alumni, students, parents, and supporters to donate. You can table-sit any time of year.

SAO requires clubs to hold no more than $100 in cash and to deposit money within 24 hours of receiving it. The SGA Fiscal Policy also treats late fundraising deposits and cash over $100 as violations. There is a safe at the Campus Center front desk for storing money outside of Boynton business hours.

  1. Fill out the Cash Sale Form on the Controller's Office forms page, or ask the Bursar's Office for a paper one.
  2. Bring the form with the cash and checks to the Bursar's Office on the 2nd floor of Boynton Hall. Use a separate form for cash and for checks.
  3. For checks, also bring a spreadsheet with the bank name, who wrote each check, the amount, and the check number.
Campus

Campus offices and policies

SGA cannot fund anything that breaks University policy, so it helps to know who to ask.

Campus offices
OfficeContact forEmail
Student Activities Office (SAO)Club administration and policy questions, Workday questions, travel, contracts, fundraisersgr-sao@wpi.edu
Events OfficeEvent planning, external speakers, sound amplification, food trucksgr-events@wpi.edu
ChartwellsCatering and external food exemption waiverscatering@wpi.edu

Helpful pages: Student Club Resources and Event Planning Resources.

  • Travel: trips that use University funds must be registered with SAO. Register three weeks ahead if the trip goes beyond a 120-mile radius of Worcester, is overnight, uses hired or rented transportation, needs a contract, or has more than 20 participants. Overnight trips or trips beyond 120 miles need a WPI faculty or staff member attending, and everyone completes an online liability form. Activities with inherent risk have to be cleared by the Director of Student Activities.
  • Gift cards: clubs can give them out at events, but must collect the date purchased, the winner's name and student ID, the amount, and the type of card. Submit that on a separate sheet with the expense report.
  • Raffles and fundraisers: register every one with SAO using the fundraiser form above.
  • Contracts: booking an artist, speaker, or novelty act needs a contract signed by an SAO staff member. SAO can provide one if the performer does not have their own.
  • Publicity: chalk only on horizontal outdoor surfaces, and remove it within a week or face a $45 minimum cleaning charge per location. Flyers in academic buildings go on cork boards only, residence hall postings need Residential Services approval, and Campus Center postings go through the Administrative Office. No painting or marking glass, except in the Campus Center with the Director's approval.
  • External speakers: anyone who is not a current WPI student, faculty, or staff member. Get written permission from the Events Office at least 14 days ahead, including the speaker's bio, the date, time, and location, expected attendance, and your promotional materials. Permission cannot be transferred to another speaker or club, and your club is responsible for any facility, security, or damage costs. Sound amplification needs advance approval too.
  • Food: Chartwells is the contracted food provider, and food orders go through them (online through Carved and Crafted). Any food not from Chartwells, including groceries and outside catering, needs a Chartwells exemption waiver, requested two weeks before the event through catering@wpi.edu.
  • Vendors over $10,000: a single vendor of $10,000 or more follows a planning timeline that starts at least 6 months before the event. Ask SAO for the current timeline.
