Club Classifications

What each of the eight classes can request from SGA, and how to reclassify.

A club's classification decides what kinds of requests SGA can fund for it. Classifications are organizational tools and are not tiers, so they say nothing about how important a club is. New clubs start out as Class VI (Provisional).

Reclassification

Changing your classification

  • You can request a reclassification between 12 and 18 months after your club was recognized by SOC. Miss the 18 month mark and your club moves to Class VII, which can still be reclassified.
  • Fill out the Reclassification Request Form on SGA's myWPI page (Surveys tab).
  • Provisional clubs can ask Financial Board for the items they expect to have in a budget during their first year. That helps you get a budget faster once you are reclassified.
Reference

Terms you will see

  • Annual item: something your club is very likely to buy every year, like league or national registration, supplies for a traditional annual event, or travel and lodging for an annual conference.
  • Non-annual item: a one-time purchase or anything not bought yearly, like reusable equipment, items with a very different cost each year, or money for events that may not happen every year.
  • Budgeted organization: a club that is SOC recognized, SAO approved, in Class I, II, or III, and has gone through the budgeting process.
  • Non-budgeted organization: a club that gets no annual funding because it is not SOC recognized, not SAO approved, received a $0 budget, or is in Class IV through VIII.
  • Approp: the Appropriations Committee, which can hear funding requests at the Treasurer's discretion. These are usually smaller, simple requests with no presenters.
  • FRA: the Fiscal Responsibility and Assistance Committee.
  • Funding Request (FR): a request for funds submitted to SGA by a recognized club.
  • Funding Request Report Form (FRRF): the form you fill out to get reimbursed for money spent on an approved funding request.
  • Food meeting: an internal club meeting where food is given to the general body and does not contribute to the purpose of the club or event.
  • Student Activities Office (SAO): the office responsible for the activities and events of recognized clubs and organizations.
  • Student Organization Council (SOC): the committee that decides whether a new club meets the requirements to be recognized.
