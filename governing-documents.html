Governing Documents

The rules SGA runs on — public, and open to amendment by the Senate.

Under review. Senate is currently discussing a restructuring of SGA’s governing documents. Proposals are debated in open session — come to a Tuesday meeting to weigh in before anything is ratified.

Amendments

How the rules change

Changes to SGA’s governing documents are proposed, debated, and voted on in Senate — in open session, where any student can watch or speak.

Quorum is a simple majority for most business, and two-thirds for financial matters.

Questions

Who interprets them

The Judicial Branch, led by the Chief Justice, interprets the governing documents when their meaning or application is disputed.
