Test Your Knowledge

Thirty flashcards on timelines, policy, and how SGA works.

Think you know SGA financials? This is a deck of 30 flashcards on funding timelines, the Fiscal Policy, budgeting, club classes, and how SGA elections work. Read the question, answer it in your head, then click the card to flip it. Use Next for the following card, or the left and right arrow keys.

  1. Funding Request timelineYour event needs money by Friday, November 20 (your Funds Needed By date). What is the latest you should submit your FR?

    November 6

    FRs should be in at least 2 weeks before your Funds Needed By date, so November 6. Financial Board tries to hear every club before its date, but it is not guaranteed, so earlier is better.

  2. Funding Request timelineWhen does the Treasurer email your hearing date and tell you whether you are in Appropriations or Financial Board?

    Sunday evening, usually about 2 weeks before your Funds Needed By date

    You get that email on a Sunday evening, usually about 2 weeks before your Funds Needed By date.

  3. Funding Request timelineYour club wants funding for one line item, merch, well under $1,000. Which group is likely to hear the request, and do you attend?

    Appropriations. You send your presentation only and do not attend.

    The Treasurer decides which group hears your request, and you can ask to be heard in Financial Board instead. Simple requests with one or a few line items, usually under $1,000, go to Appropriations. Complex requests with multiple line items, usually larger amounts, go to Financial Board, where you present in person for 5 minutes or less.

  4. Funding Request timelineYour hearing is on Wednesday. When are your slides and cost breakdown due to the FRA Chair?

    Tuesday by 5pm

    Tuesday by 5pm. Late submissions will not be considered.

  5. Funding Request timelineWhen and where does Financial Board meet?

    Wednesdays, 6 to 8pm, CC Taylor Room

    Wednesdays from 6 to 8pm in the Campus Center Taylor Room. If your request is heard in Appropriations, you do not attend at all.

  6. Funding Request timelineYou presented at Financial Board on Wednesday. When does your decision email usually show up?

    Thursday night

    Thursday night.

  7. After the decisionYour FR was denied and you want to appeal. What is the timeline and who do you contact?

    24 hours from the decision email. An executive officer of your club emails the SGA Treasurer.

    You have 24 hours from your decision email, and an executive officer emails the SGA Treasurer with the reason. Appeals are usually heard by Senate or the Executive Board, and that decision is final.

  8. After the decisionYour FR was approved with stipulations and your event is next week. Can you start spending?

    No, not until you meet or email with the financial senator assigned to your request

    You have to go over the stipulations with your assigned financial senator first. If you spend before that, the request will not be reimbursed.

  9. After the decisionWhat is the deadline to submit the Funding Request Report Form (FRRF)?

    No later than 1 week after your last purchase

    No later than one week after your last purchase, after everything is paid for.

  10. After the decisionYour FR was approved for $100. You ended up spending $150. How much do you get back?

    $100

    $100. You get back what you actually spent, up to the approved amount. If you had spent $50, you would get $50.

  11. After the decisionYour FRRF is approved. About how long can the reimbursement take to reach your club's Workday account?

    Up to 3 weeks

    Up to 3 weeks, because the IDT has to go through WPI Accounting.

  12. Budgeting timelineWhich term is the budgeting workshop held in, and who has to attend?

    C-term, a representative from the club's executive board

    C-term, and a representative from your club's executive board needs to attend before you can submit.

  13. Budgeting timelineRoughly when does the budget portal open, and when do clubs get their budgets back?

    Opens in January and stays open about a month, budgets back in early March

    The portal opens in January and stays open for about a month. The Appropriations Committee returns budgets in early March.

  14. Budgeting timelineWhen can you start spending from an approved budget?

    At the start of the fiscal year, July 1

    The fiscal year runs July through June, and your budget is available July 1.

  15. Budgeting timelineWhich classes can submit an annual budget?

    Class I, II, and III

    Only Class I, II, and III organizations.

  16. Budgeting timelineHalfway through the year you want to move money between line items in your budget. What is the timeline?

    Submit the reallocation form at least 2 weeks before the funds are needed

    An executive officer submits the Budget Reallocation Request Form at least 2 weeks before the funds are needed, and the Treasurer decides whether to hear it. No report form is needed after it is approved.

  17. Classes and reclassificationA club was just recognized by the Student Organization Council. What class is it in, and when can it apply to be reclassified?

    Class VI (Provisional), after 12 months

    New clubs start as Class VI (Provisional). They can apply for reclassification after 12 months.

  18. Classes and reclassificationA provisional club never applies for reclassification and hits 18 months. What happens?

    It becomes Class VII (Non-Funded), and can still apply for reclassification

    It becomes Class VII, which is non-funded. It can apply for reclassification, but it cannot submit a Funding Request until it does.

  19. Fiscal Policy scenariosYour club signed a contract with a caterer yesterday and now wants SGA to cover it through an FR. Can SGA hear the request?

    No, SGA cannot hear requests where you already signed a contract or spent the money

    SGA can only fund things that have not happened yet. It cannot hear requests for events or purchases that already happened, or where the club has already signed a contract.

  20. Fiscal Policy scenariosYou want club shirts that cost $40 each, for members only. What is the most SGA can fund per shirt, and how many merch items can your club get per year?

    $20 per shirt, one merch item per fiscal year

    Merch is funded up to $25 or 50% per item, whichever is lower. 50% of $40 is $20, so that is the cap. It is one merch item per club per fiscal year, for internal members only, bought in a single bulk order. Shipping is funded in full.

  21. Fiscal Policy scenariosYour club wants to fly 8 members to a national conference. Can SGA help with the flights?

    No, SGA does not fund student airfare (registration and lodging can still be funded up to 60%)

    SGA does not fund student airfare, except cargo airfare for essential equipment. It can cover up to 60% of registration and lodging and up to 100% of land transportation.

  22. Fiscal Policy scenariosWhich clubs can get funding for a banquet, and how often?

    Class III only, one per fiscal year, up to $30 per expected attendee (not counting food)

    Banquets are for Class III clubs only, one per fiscal year, up to $30 per expected attendee, not counting food.

  23. Fiscal Policy scenariosYour club plans a raffle and wants SGA to pay for the raffle items. What happens?

    SGA does not fund raffle items or gift items. Prizes won by skill are funded up to $1 per expected attendee.

    Raffle items and gifts are not funded. Prize items that are won by skill can be funded up to $1 per expected attendee per event.

  24. Fiscal Policy scenariosHow many retreats can a Class I, II, or III club get funded per fiscal year?

    Up to 2, with at most 1 off campus

    Up to 2 per fiscal year, with a maximum of 1 off campus (it has to add value compared to an on-campus retreat). Retreats limited to executive members are not funded.

  25. Fiscal Policy scenariosYour event sells tickets and brings in $500. What has to happen with that money?

    It has to go toward offsetting the SGA-funded part of the event

    All income from ticket sales has to go toward offsetting the SGA-funded part of the event.

  26. SGA elections and termsWhen do the Executive Branch elections (President, Vice President, Treasurer, Secretary) begin?

    First Monday of B-term, running until the following Friday at 5pm

    They begin on the first Monday of B-term and run until the following Friday at 5pm.

  27. SGA elections and termsWhen do Senator elections begin?

    Third Monday of B-term, running until the following Friday at 5pm

    They begin on the third Monday of B-term and run until the following Friday at 5pm.

  28. SGA elections and termsWhen does an elected Executive Branch member's term start and end?

    From the first regular Senate meeting of C-term after their election, through the first Senate meeting of the following C-term

    They serve from the first regular Senate meeting of C-term after the election through the first Senate meeting of the following C-term. In between, they are "elect" (for example, Treasurer-elect).

  29. SGA elections and termsWhat is the Treasurer-elect supposed to do during the transition?

    Assist the Treasurer with preparation for the budgeting process

    Among their duties, the Treasurer-elect assists the Treasurer with preparing for the budgeting process. They also join orientation and attend Executive and Senate meetings.

  30. SGA elections and termsSenate approves a change to policy. How much notice do affected organizations get before it takes effect?

    At least 7 days

    Affected organizations have to be officially notified no less than 7 days before the change goes into effect.
