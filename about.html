Our mission

The official voice of the student body

SGA exists to advocate for the students of Worcester Polytechnic Institute, to ensure that their rights are respected, to act as the official voice of the student body, to represent students’ interests in all matters, and to enact legislation with these goals in mind.

In practice that means three things at once: we sit across the table from administration when policy is written, we fund and support the organizations that make campus life worth living, and we give every undergraduate a standing, public place to raise a concern.

We are committed to inclusivity, engagement, and leadership — and to collaborating with WPI’s administration rather than shouting past it.

What we do

The work, concretely

Represent

Senators carry student concerns into the room where policy gets decided — academic, residential, financial, and everything in between.

Fund

The Financial Board allocates the student activities budget across recognized organizations, events, and initiatives.

Legislate

Senate debates and passes legislation governing SGA and the organizations it recognizes, in open session.

Support

Committees run programming, outreach, and fiscal education so clubs can actually use the resources available to them.

Interpret

The Judicial Branch settles questions about what the constitution and bylaws mean and how they apply.

Listen

Public comment opens every Senate meeting. Anyone can speak — no membership, no agenda slot, no prior notice.

Three branches

How SGA is organized

The Executive, Legislative, and Judicial branches work together to serve the student body.

01

Executive

Five elected officers set priorities, run day-to-day operations, and represent students to administration.

02

Legislative

The Senate and six standing committees: Student Affairs, Community Outreach, Appropriations, Fiscal Responsibility and Assistance, Fiscal Education, and Marketing.

03

Judicial

Chaired by the Chief Justice, the Judicial Branch interprets SGA’s governing documents.

Senate meetings are open to all

Senate meets weekly on Tuesdays at 6:00 PM in Innovation Studio 203. Everyone is welcome to voice concerns, ask questions, or comment at the start of the meeting — you do not need to be an SGA member to speak.
