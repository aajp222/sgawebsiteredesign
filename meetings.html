Meetings & Minutes

Senate meets weekly, in the open, and publishes the record.

Weekly

Senate meeting

Tuesdays · 6:00 PM
Innovation Studio, Room 203

Open to all students, faculty, and staff. You do not need to be an SGA member to attend.

Every meeting

Public comment

Public comment opens each meeting. Anyone can voice a concern, ask a question, or comment before Senate moves to business.

No sign-up required — arrive at 6:00 PM and speak.

Bring something to Senate

How to get on the agenda

  1. For a quick concern, use public comment

    Show up at 6:00 PM and speak during the public comment period. Nothing to prepare.

  2. For a formal item, email the Secretary

    Agenda items and legislation go through the Secretary ahead of the meeting.

    sgasecretary@wpi.edu

  3. For funding, go through the Financial Board

    Funding requests follow their own process rather than the Senate agenda.
