Ways in

Four routes into SGA

Attend

Sit in on a Tuesday Senate meeting and use public comment. Costs you an hour.

Committee

Join a standing committee — Student Affairs, Outreach, Marketing, or one of the financial committees.

Honorary Senator

Take a non-voting seat and do the work of a Senator while you learn the process.

Run

Stand for election as a Senator or for the Executive Board.

Before you commit

Attend weekly Senate meetings, debate and vote on legislation, serve on a committee, and carry concerns from your part of campus into the room where policy gets decided.
