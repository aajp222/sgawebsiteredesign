How it works

Three branches, one student body

SGA is structured like a government because it does the work of one — representation, legislation, and interpretation.

01

Executive

The President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Chief Justice set priorities, run operations, and act as the student body’s liaison to WPI administration.

02

Legislative

The Senate and its standing committees debate and pass legislation, allocate funding, and represent every corner of campus life.

03

Judicial

Led by the Chief Justice, the Judicial Branch interprets the constitution and bylaws and resolves disputes over how they apply.
