Seats

Ways to hold a seat

Senator

Voting members of the Senate. Senators debate and vote on legislation, sit on committees, and bring student concerns forward.

Honorary Senator non-voting

Participate fully in discussion and committee work without a vote — the usual way in for students joining mid-term.

Committee Chair

Lead one of the six standing committees, sit on the Cabinet, and hold a seat in Senate.

Organizational Representative

Recognized student organizations send a representative to Senate to speak for their membership.

Executive Board non-voting

The President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Chief Justice sit in Senate without a vote.

Appointed positions

Tech Coordinator, Accountant, and Financial Board Secretary are appointed rather than elected.

Getting in

How to become a member

  1. Show up to a meeting

    Senate meets Tuesdays at 6:00 PM in Innovation Studio 203. No registration, no application — attending is the first step and costs you nothing.

  2. Talk to the Vice President

    The VP manages Senate operations and committee assignments and can tell you exactly which seats and committees have openings right now.

    sgavp@wpi.edu

  3. Join a committee

    Committee work is where most of SGA’s substance happens, and it is open before you ever hold a seat.

  4. Run in an election

    Senator and Executive Board positions are filled by election. Watch @wpi.sga for nomination deadlines.

Not sure where you fit?

Email the Vice President, or just walk into a Tuesday meeting and ask. Both work.
