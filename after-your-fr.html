After Your FR

What each decision means, how to appeal, and how to get reimbursed.

You have a decision on your Funding Request. This page covers what each decision means, how to appeal, and how to get reimbursed. If you have not submitted a request yet, start with Funding Requests.

Decisions

What your decision means

What each Funding Request decision means
DecisionWhat it means for you
Approved in fullThe full amount is approved. You can start spending as soon as you get the decision.
Approved with stipulationsThe amount is approved, but you cannot spend it until you meet or email with the financial senator assigned to your request to go over the stipulations and make the changes Financial Board asked for. If you spend first, the request will not be reimbursed.
Approved in an amended amountA different (usually lower) amount is approved and you can use it right away. This usually means part of the request did not line up with the Fiscal Policy or the standards, or the board did not think that part added to the event. You can appeal.
TabledNot approved or denied yet, and it can be heard again later. Do not spend any money. A financial senator will work with you to improve the request, and you can ask to have it un-tabled. Common reasons: the board wanted clarifications or changes, the cost breakdown was unclear, outside funding or unrestricted club money had not been explored, or there was not enough sign of student interest.
DeniedThe funds are not approved and you cannot spend the money. Usual reasons: the request did not line up with the Fiscal Policy, did not fit your club's purpose, or was outside what the Student Life Fee is meant for. You can appeal.
Appeals

Appealing a decision

You have 24 hours from your decision email. An executive officer of your club emails the SGA Treasurer at sgatreasurer@wpi.edu with the reason for the appeal.

The Treasurer decides whether to hear it and tells Senate at the next meeting, and Senate can overturn that with a two-thirds vote. Appeals are usually heard by Senate or the Executive Board, and the decision on an appeal is final.

Reimbursement

Getting reimbursed

Funding Requests work as reimbursements. You spend the approved money first, then submit a Funding Request Report Form (FRRF) on myWPI. Once the FRA Chair approves it, SGA sends an IDT (internal department transfer) to your club's Workday account.

Step 1

Spend

Stay within your approved amount, both overall and for each line item. Meet any stipulations first.

Step 2

Report

Submit the report form after everything is paid for, and no later than one week after your last purchase.

Step 3

Wait

IDTs have to go through WPI Accounting, so allow up to 3 weeks.

You get back what you actually spent, up to the approved amount. Approved for $100 and spent $50 means you get $50. Approved for $100 and spent $150 means you get $100. Anything that was not in your original request will not be funded, so email the FRA Chair before you buy if plans change. Submit the report form even if you spent nothing or the event was cancelled, because we need it for our records. Any money that was approved but not spent goes back to the SGA reserve for the following fiscal year.

Your club account can go negative while you make purchases and reimburse students who paid on the club's behalf. That is fine as long as you stay within the approved amount. No club account can have a negative balance at the end of the fiscal year, so make sure your reimbursement comes through.

Details

Report form and proof of purchase

  1. Sign into myWPI with your WPI credentials.
  2. Search "Student Government Association" and open the group page.
  3. Select the Surveys tab.
  4. Open the Funding Request Report Form for the current fiscal year.
  5. Fill it out. It asks for your request ID number (in the email from the SGA Treasurer confirming your request, and in the SGA Financials Notion), the date the request was approved, the amount approved, and the total you spent. The total can be above or below the approved amount, but only count purchases that fall within your original request.
  6. Attach documentation for everything you spent: IDTs, check requests, receipts, invoices, and payment confirmations. Submit photos or scans of receipts and PDFs of invoices and payments.

Make your breakdown easy to follow. The breakdown template with receipts shows what to submit. You do not have to copy it exactly as long as it has the same information.

If you are having trouble getting any documentation, email the FRA Chair at sgafrachair@wpi.edu and let them know.

Every item needs proper proof of purchase to be reimbursed. If you cannot show it (a lost receipt, for example), the item cannot be funded.

What counts as proof of purchase
Who paidWhat you need
A student made the purchaseA receipt showing part of the student's personal card number (the last four digits, for example). If you do not have an adequate receipt, a matching Expense Report or Student Non-Employee Ad-Hoc Form (from the WPI website) that is visible in Workday can be used instead.
WPI faculty or staff made the purchaseA matching Expense Report submitted by that faculty or staff member, visible in Workday.
You were sent an invoiceA matching Supplier Invoice, visible in Workday.
The event sold ticketsIf you used University Tickets, a copy of the report from SAO. If not, your club's own record of tickets sold.

Everything on Workday, purchasing, expense reports, and IDTs is on the Treasurer Toolkit page.
