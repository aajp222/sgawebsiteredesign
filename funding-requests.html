Funding Requests

Ask SGA for money for a specific event or purchase, and know what to expect at every step.

A Funding Request (FR) is how your club asks SGA for money for a specific event or purchase. SGA reimburses you after you spend it. This page covers everything up to your hearing.

Submit an FR on myWPI After your FR
Timeline

How it works

Funding Request timeline
WhenWhat happens
At least 2 weeks before your Funds Needed By dateYou submit your FR on myWPI.
Sunday eveningThe SGA Treasurer emails your hearing date and whether you are in Appropriations or Financial Board. This usually reaches you about 2 weeks before your Funds Needed By date.
Tuesday, by 5pmSend your presentation and cost breakdown to the FRA Chair. Late submissions will not be considered.
Wednesday, 6 to 8pmFinancial Board meets in the CC Taylor Room. If you are in Appropriations, you do not attend.
Thursday nightYour decision email arrives.
After you spend the moneySubmit the Funding Request Report Form to get reimbursed.

Financial Board tries to hear every club before its Funds Needed By date, but it is not guaranteed. The earlier you submit, the better your odds.

Hearings

Where will your request be heard?

The Treasurer decides which group hears your request. You can also ask to be heard in Financial Board.

Lighter requests

Appropriations

One or a few line items (merch is the most common), usually under $1,000.

  • You send your presentation only
  • You do not attend
Bigger requests

Financial Board

Complex requests with multiple line items, usually larger amounts.

  • You present in person, 5 minutes or less
  • The financial senators ask questions
  • You leave while the board discusses and votes
The details

Everything else you need to know

  • FRs are for one-time costs like big events, trips, or merch.
  • You can also use one for a normally budgeted item if you will go over that line item or have no budget for it.
  • Class I, II, III, and VI clubs can submit any FR. Class IV, V, and VIII clubs need their FR to benefit the entire undergraduate student body. Class VII is unfunded and cannot submit. Not sure of your class? See Club Classifications.
  • SGA can only fund things that have not happened yet. We cannot hear a request for something you already bought or already signed a contract for.
  • The "Funds Needed By" date is the day the money has to be available, which is not always the date of your event.

If these are missing, the Treasurer can decide not to hear your request.

  • A cost breakdown with specific line items, quantities, and any other money going toward the cost (your budget, outside funding, and so on).
  • Up-to-date quotes for anything you are asking SGA to pay for.
  • Approval from the right campus office, shown in your presentation. Events need the Events Office. Travel and external speakers or performers need SAO. Food that does not come from Chartwells needs a signed Chartwells exemption form.

Office contacts and forms are on the Treasurer Toolkit page.

  1. Sign into myWPI with your WPI credentials.
  2. Search "Student Government Association" and open the group page.
  3. Select the Surveys tab.
  4. Open the Funding Requests form for the current fiscal year (it is named like "FY27 Funding Requests").
  5. Fill out the form using the list below.

What the form asks for:

  • Name of event or initiative. If it has no official name, one to five words describing it is fine.
  • Funding requested. The total cost minus any other funding you already have. If you are also getting money from Graduate Student Government, include that amount.
  • How the funds will be used. A few sentences on the event or item and why your club needs it.
  • Breakdown of funds. Specific line items, quantities, and any other funding sources.

Keep it to 5 minutes or less. Cover what the request is for, why, and a detailed cost breakdown. Include quotes and screenshots of your approvals. Use the FR presentation template, and email it to the FRA Chair at sgafrachair@wpi.edu by Tuesday at 5pm.

Events

  • Where, and whether the Events Office approved it (include your 25Live reservation link)
  • When: dates and times
  • Registration: tickets in advance or at the door, and the price
  • Expected attendance (undergraduate and graduate), and previous attendance if it has run before
  • Your advertising plan, on campus and digital
  • Approvals, such as a Chartwells exemption for outside food or SAO approval for external speakers, with a screenshot of each
  • Quotes for services, food, and anything else

Equipment

  • Where you will store it, and whether you have approval to store it there
  • Expected lifespan before it needs replacing or repair
  • How many the club already has, and why SGA should fund more
  • Screenshots of relevant approvals (such as Club Sports approval for Club Sports equipment) and of all quotes, including installation

Merch and apparel

  • Total quantity and the funds requested per item
  • Whether the design includes the WPI logo or the initials WPI, and whether WPI Marketing approved it (include a screenshot)
  • Expected interest, and previous interest if you have run this before
  • A picture of the design, front and back

Travel

  • Where you are going, when, and whether SAO approved it
  • How many undergraduates, graduate students, and advisors are going, and previous attendance if applicable
  • How attendees are chosen
  • Screenshots of all approvals. The SAO travel policy is summarized in University Policies on the Treasurer Toolkit page.

The board weighs the Fiscal Policy, some general standards, and the criteria for your type of request. The standards are not official policy. They describe what the current board tends to care about, and the board uses discretion on each request.

Does it fit the spirit of the Fiscal Policy?

  • Fits the purpose of your club
  • Impact compared to cost, and cost per student
  • Efficient choices that make the money go a long way
  • Spreads the Student Life Fee fairly, in ways that reach students across campus

Is it fiscally responsible?

  • Quality of items, including renting when it is cheaper, using reusable campus resources, and buying durable items
  • Quality of planning: have you thought through every part of the purchase?
  • Past success: how did the same or a similar event go before?
  • Outside funding: have you looked into other funding and discounts?

Does it fit the mission of SGA?

  • Collaboration with other clubs and involvement with the community
  • A good representation of WPI
  • A good cause that helps people and contributes to the community
Fiscal Policy

What SGA can and cannot fund

This summarizes the SGA Fiscal Policy adopted April 28th, 2026. The full document is on the Governing Documents page and is the official source if anything here looks different.

Fiscal Policy summary
TopicWhat the policy says
AlcoholSGA funds cannot be used for alcohol or illegal substances.
Already spentSGA cannot hear requests for events or purchases that already happened, or where the club has signed a contract.
Who it must benefitSGA funds are for the undergraduate student body. Graduate students or others can attend only if that does not raise the SGA-funded cost or limit undergraduate access.
Cost to attendSGA-sponsored activities must be free for WPI undergraduates. The exception is ticketed events (see Tickets).
Travel and feesUp to 60% of application fees, registration fees, and lodging. Up to 100% of land transportation. Personal vehicles can be reimbursed for fuel based on documented distance. Class II clubs can get the greater of 60% or $60 for land transportation. SGA does not fund student airfare, except cargo airfare for essential equipment.
Dues60% of individual dues, 100% of annual organizational dues, and 50% of individual facility membership costs required to participate.
AdvisorsIf SAO requires an advisor for the event, their application fee, lodging, and registration can be funded in full, with documentation of the requirement.
ConferencesEither 20 members to a regional conference and 10 to a national conference, or 20 members to a national conference, per fiscal year (not both options). If more members want to go than can be funded, use an objective, documented selection method.
RetreatsUp to 2 per fiscal year for Class I, II, and III clubs, with a maximum of 1 off campus (it must add value compared to an on-campus retreat). Food up to the Worcester per diem dinner rate per person, and 60% of other costs. Your request must state the purpose and how attendees are selected. Retreats limited to executive members are not funded.
BanquetsClass III clubs only. One per fiscal year, up to $30 per expected attendee, not counting food.
ApparelClub merchandise: up to $25 or 50% per item, whichever is lower, with shipping funded in full. One merchandise item per club per fiscal year, for internal members only, bought in a single bulk order. Uniforms can be funded in full.
FoodUp to the Worcester per diem dinner rate, and only for certain cases: food clubs holding a food meeting, cultural or religious events open to all undergraduates hosted by a cultural or religious club, events expecting 15% or more of the undergraduate student body, water at on-campus events, club-run food machines, cost of doing business, professional networking events, retreats, and banquets. For cultural or religious events, your request has to explain how the food connects to the event's purpose.
PrizesPrize items (won by skill) up to $1 per expected attendee per event.
GiveawaysClass I clubs up to $1 per expected attendee per event. Class III clubs up to $5. Not funded for other classes.
Raffles and giftsSGA does not fund raffle items or gift items.
TicketsAll income from ticket sales has to go toward offsetting the SGA-funded part of the event.
FundraisingA donation cannot be required to access any SGA-funded part of a fundraising event.
AttendanceFunded events have to track attendance, and your request has to include the per-person cost (total projected cost divided by projected attendance).
RevenueIf your event brings in income, any gap between expected and actual income is on your club to cover.
