FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Funding Requests are submitted through the form on myWPI. The Funding Requests page walks through each step and what to have ready.

Financial Board meets on Wednesdays from 6 to 8 pm in the Campus Center Taylor Room during hearing weeks.

You will get an email on the Sunday evening about two weeks before your Funds Needed By date. It has your hearing details.

Hearings run Wednesday from 6 to 8 pm. Decisions come out Thursday night.

Send your slides to the FRA Chair by 5 pm on the Tuesday before your hearing. See Funding Requests for the full timeline.

You can appeal within 24 hours of the decision email. Appeals usually go to Senate or Exec, and that decision is final. Details are on After Your FR.

Stipulations are conditions attached to an approved request, such as a lower amount or specific items only. Your decision email lists them, and you need to follow them to get reimbursed.

Submit an FRRF with your receipts after you spend the money. Steps and accepted proof of purchase are on After Your FR.

Start with the Budgeting page, then email the Appropriations Chair at sgaappropchair@wpi.edu if you are still stuck.

Financial Board handles all of SGA's financials. The SGA Treasurer heads it, and it is made up of senators who serve in these roles and committees:

  • Fiscal Responsibility and Assistance (FRA): makes sure SGA funds are spent appropriately and helps organizations spend according to policy.
  • Appropriations: makes hearing Funding Requests more efficient by meeting before Financial Board to recommend outcomes.
  • Fiscal Education (FED): educates senators and student organizations so procedure and policy are followed.
  • Financial Justice (appointed): the final interpreter of financial legislation during Financial Board meetings.
  • Accountant (appointed): keeps the financial records of spending for each organization.
  • Financial Board Secretary (appointed): takes and shares minutes for Appropriations and Financial Board meetings.

Financial Board and Appropriations both meet on Wednesdays. The Fiscal Policy lays out the criteria used for funding decisions.
