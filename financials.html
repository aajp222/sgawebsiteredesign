Financials

SGA allocates the student activities budget. Here is how your organization gets a piece of it.

Funding

Requesting funding

Recognized student organizations can request funding through the Financial Board.

  1. Get your budget together

    Itemize what you need and what it costs. The Fiscal Education committee runs training for club treasurers if this is your first time.

    sgafedchair@wpi.edu

  2. Submit to the Financial Board

    The Financial Board, chaired by the Treasurer, reviews requests from recognized organizations.

    sgatreasurer@wpi.edu

  3. Appropriations reviews it

    The Appropriations committee evaluates the request and makes a recommendation.

    sgaappropchair@wpi.edu

  4. The Board votes

    Financial matters require a two-thirds quorum. Once approved, Fiscal Responsibility and Assistance can help you manage the allocation.

    sgafrachair@wpi.edu

Deadlines and forms vary by term. Email sgatreasurer@wpi.edu for the current cycle, or watch @wpi.sga for announcements.

Financial Services cluster

Who handles the money

Financial Board

Makes funding decisions for recognized student organizations. Chaired by the Treasurer. Two-thirds quorum required on financial matters.

Appropriations

Reviews funding requests and recommends allocations to the Board.

sgaappropchair@wpi.edu

Fiscal Responsibility & Assistance

Helps organizations manage their budgets and stay in good standing.

sgafrachair@wpi.edu

Fiscal Education

Trains club treasurers and leaders on how SGA funding works before they need it.

sgafedchair@wpi.edu
